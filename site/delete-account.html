Delete your GymBuddy account

You can delete your account and all your data yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

In the app

  1. Open GymBuddy and log in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom right).
  3. Tap Delete Account, enter your password, and confirm.

Deleting is permanent. Tip: tap Export Training History first if you want a copy.

Without the app

If you cannot open the app (for example you uninstalled it or forgot your password), email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my GymBuddy account". We will delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email and password).
  • Your profile: name, age, days, goal, equipment and units.
  • Every set you logged, every finished workout and record, your custom routine and your machine settings.

Crash reports hold no personal details, only an anonymous account number, so there is nothing in them to delete. Emails we already sent are in your inbox, not with us.
