Your training starts here

Walk into the gym knowing exactly what to do.

GymBuddy is a gym guide for people who are new or returning. Answer a few questions, get a simple three-day plan, and follow it set by set: how to set up each machine, what a good rep looks like, how long to rest, and what to lift next time.

  • A plan that fits you: built from your days per week, goal and equipment.
  • Setup cues and form demos for every exercise, so nothing is a guess.
  • A rest timer that alerts you on time, even with the phone locked.
  • Works with no signal: your sets are saved on the phone and upload later.
  • Kilograms or pounds, your choice.

GymBuddy is for Android, and iPhone is planned. It is free while it is being tested.
