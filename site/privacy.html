Privacy policy

Your training data is yours.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what the GymBuddy app collects, why, and the choices you have. GymBuddy is run by [OPERATOR NAME AND COUNTRY]. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider).
  • Your training profile: the name you enter, your age, how many days a week you train, your goal, your equipment, and whether you prefer kilograms or pounds.
  • Your training: every set you log (exercise, weight, reps, time), your finished workouts and totals, personal records, your custom routine, and the seat and pad settings you save for machines.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a technical report is sent to help us fix it. It contains the app version, phone model and Android version, the error, and an anonymous account number. It does not contain your email, name, or workout data.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising ID. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

To run the app: sign you in, build and rotate your plan, save and show your history and records, and send the emails you ask for (confirming your address, resetting a password, changing your email). Crash reports are used only to find and fix bugs.

Where it is kept and who handles it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in. Your data is stored there, protected so that each account can only read its own rows.
  • MailerSend delivers the emails the app sends you.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, as described above.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. While you are offline your sets are kept on your phone and upload when you are back online. The Bro Card images you create are made on your phone and only leave it if you share them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: to sync your data.
  • Notifications and foreground service: for the rest timer, so it can count down on the lock screen and alert you when rest is over.
  • Vibration and keep awake: a buzz when rest ends, and the screen stays on during a workout.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, sets, workouts and machine settings are removed from our database right away (see Delete your account).

Your choices

  • See and export: Profile → Export Training History saves everything we hold about your training as a file.
  • Correct: change your name, age, days, goal, equipment and units in Profile → Training profile.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete Account, or email us. See Delete your account.
  • If you live in the EU or UK you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Health information

GymBuddy is a training guide, not medical advice. What you log is about exercise only, and it is not used to diagnose or treat anything.

Children

GymBuddy is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
